# Voice delivery: provider behavior and platform decisions

Reviewed against official documentation October 13, 2026 (Fish Drama3); Inworld review September 23, 2026. This file supersedes dated explanatory comments when they describe an older provider behavior. The implementation and request receipts remain the evidence for what actually runs.

## Inworld

The platform requests `inworld-tts-2`, which supports natural-language delivery instructions. Its `temperature` field is ignored; `deliveryMode` is the control for variability. Lively maps to `CREATIVE`, Balanced to `BALANCED`, and Steady to `STABLE`. Lively is our default after Kade preferred its engagement over the Balanced trial. A saved client choice wins. Speed remains a separate setting. Flash does not support delivery steering, so changing to Flash would lose a capability this platform needs.

A direction lasts until replaced or reset; punctuation, paragraph breaks, and sound events do not end it. We lift the opening direction into `instruction`, retain actual mid-text changes inline, and repeat the current instruction only when we create a separate provider request. Inworld prefers one mechanism per request; mixing the field and inline tags is nevertheless explicitly defined. Our mixed use preserves existing authored changes without extra splits or rewriting speech. Do not add a direction to every sentence.

`synthesisContext.previousRequests` supplies previous synthesized **text**, in order. It is not billed, and has a 2,000-character total limit. We keep at most three recent pieces within 1,900 characters for 90 seconds. Cross-request context is scoped to seat and resolved voice, previews never read or write it, and a request takes a fixed snapshot. Completion order cannot reverse the history. Failed requests are not remembered. Overlapping prefetches may lack a still-in-flight predecessor; this cache does not supply user audio or a full conversation. Old clients do not send a conversation ID, so the same voice on one seat may still share recent context across chats within the expiry window.

The clone/reference strongly affects habitual delivery. Lively permits variation; it does not by itself understand why a line should sound relieved, skeptical, or affectionate. Authored directions should describe the speaker's attitude toward the actual words. Emotional warmth does not require whispering, and excitement does not require shouting. Keep explicit performance requests possible. Cloning guidance differs between instant and professional clones; do not replace a person's chosen voice as a tuning shortcut.

## Fish Audio

October 14 selected catalog: the public pickers show only operator-selected reference voices. Old named, numbered, described, raw Fish and Inworld bindings remain resolvable as retained aliases. Never rewrite saved selections or reorder the legacy fallback pool when editing a picker. Private audio and exact reference words are fetched from private B2 objects, checked against committed hashes and sent inline with MessagePack `references`; no public Fish model is created. Reference words, filenames and storage credentials do not appear in `/voices.json`.

The Fish voice director (`director.js`, Oct 17 2026, Kade's yes after an a/b listen): a Fish chat piece of at least `FISH_DIRECTOR_MIN_CHARS` (140) characters is sent to `FISH_DIRECTOR_MODEL` (Gemini 3.5 Flash, thinking off) to add markup in the pattern of Fish's own Drama3 examples: one or two mid-line directions where the mood turns, one `<emphasis>`/`<stress>` on the payoff word, at most one `[pause]`, at most one `<soft>` closing line. It is kept only when every word, punctuation mark and existing tag survives and the counts hold; added sounds and tempo directions are dropped (sounds stay with talkSounds). A failure, a timeout (`FISH_DIRECTOR_TIMEOUT_MS`, 2.5 s) or doubt speaks the piece plain; 401/402/403/429 rest it for 10 minutes. Results are cached by text. Never for reading, STABLE, picker previews or the short opening piece (the iPhone's first piece is short, and later pieces are prepared while earlier ones play). `FISH_DIRECTOR=1` with `FISH_DIRECTOR_KEY` turns it on; `/health` shows `fishDirector`.

Platform authors still use hidden `%%%actor direction%%%` markers, which the speech proxy converts to Drama3 square brackets before the affected words. Brief a voice actor about attitude, intent and the listener. Put a sound where it happens; change delivery when the meaning changes. Avoid S1 parenthesis commands, repetitive sentence resets and mixing raw provider markup into conversational display text. The shared character and gateway prompts teach this format. An explicit direct Fish script can use the seven paired scopes `whisper`, `emphasis`, `soft`, `fast`, `slow`, `stress`, `forceful`; the proxy closes/reopens these at artificial request boundaries. Other XML is not a supported delivery language. Existing Inworld selections retain their adapter and instruction behavior.

The default is `drama-3`, selected explicitly in the HTTP `model` header for every Fish voice. Existing reference IDs and the shared PCM path remain the same. `FISH_TTS_MODEL=s2.1-pro` is an explicit rollback. It moves the saved Fish reference IDs only: the selected picker voices send their private reference inline and were checked on drama-3 alone, so they stay on drama-3 (logged at boot as a warning; `/health` shows `fishReferenceModel`). To move them as well, for example in a full Drama3 outage, also set `FISH_REFERENCE_MODEL` to a model you have checked with inline references. Paired emphasis follows the model each voice is actually sent to. Unknown model names are refused before synthesis, because Fish otherwise silently falls back to S2.1 Pro. Drama3 retains free-form bracket descriptions and supports scoped paired tags. House stressed words, including speech Markdown emphasis, become word-scoped `<emphasis>WORD</emphasis>` on Drama3; explicit directions, existing emphasis/stress scopes and acronym spellings stay intact. The S2 rollback retains its prefix adapter, and Inworld keeps its own emphasis behavior. S1's parenthesis dialect is different. Use sparse, compatible descriptions close to the affected words. Keep sounds separate from persistent attitude; avoid repeated cue resets and contradictory combinations.

Current production defaults: temperature 0.9, top-p 0.85, normal latency, source speed 1.0, and our existing text normalization before synthesis. Explicit delivery maps Steady/Balanced/Lively to 0.5/0.9/1.0. Fish's latency value named `balanced` is a speed/quality setting, unrelated to our Delivery picker. Keep normal latency for the quality baseline. Per-sentence reseeding stays off. The one-paragraph request boundary and bounded sentence pauses remain because earlier platform trials found repeated/gibberish output with joined paragraphs; this session fixes lost directions without discarding that evidence.

Conversation versus reading (October 16, 2026). Chat speech, meaning anything that is not a voice preview, a lab preset, a STABLE request, or a Library `reading:` session, can get two extras. First, Fish gets a voice-actor brief from `FISH_DEFAULT_DIRECTION`, which is live (October 17, her pick from four tested wordings) as "telling a close friend all about it on the phone, letting every feeling show in your voice". The brief is skipped when a request opens with the character's own direction. Second, spoken filler words, controlled by `FISH_TALK_FILLERS=1` (live) and `INWORLD_TALK_FILLERS=1` (off). Each paragraph gets at most one filler, and never in its first sentence, in lists, in quoted passages, or inside tags. The fillers change only the words sent to the provider, never the reply people read. A request's own `fillers` true/false, or the `x-kade-tts-fillers` header, overrides the server default; this is the listener's switch. Blind paired judging (gemini-4-argon, which correctly ranked a narrator decoy lower, 4 to 14) scored the brief at 18 to 7 against no brief, and the brief plus fillers at 26 to 3. Dropping the added sentence pauses, a lower temperature, and Inworld's adjective-style baseline did not help. `/health` reports `fishTalk` and `inworldTalk`. Fish temperature is 1.0 with top-p 1.0 for chat since October 17 (160 clips at four settings showed no errors). STABLE still maps to 0.5, and `FISH_TEMPERATURE_MAX` is a ceiling over every delivery.

Later on October 16 two more pieces were added. The first is little human sounds: `FISH_TALK_SOUNDS` / `INWORLD_TALK_SOUNDS`, plus a request's own `sounds` true or false. A request with `fillers` false turns them off as well. They follow these rules:
- At most one sound per paragraph, placed by the words: a small laugh right after a plainly funny line, a sigh before a tired or relieved line (never in front of "phew" or "ugh"), a breath at an opening or before a long sentence, and a throat clear rarely.
- Each engine gets its own spelling. Inworld only takes words from its sound list (`[breathe]`, `[chuckle]`, `[sigh]`, `[clear throat]`). Drama3's spellings can be changed with `FISH_SOUND_*`.
- In paired judging, `[small laugh]` after a funny line won 5 to 1. `[clear throat]` came out as a cough and lost 2 to 4, and `[quietly clears throat]` won 5 to 1.

The second is auditions. A conversation audition now counts as talk and gets the same treatment as a voice message from that voice: speed, delivery, fillers and sounds. Book scenes stay plain (`KIND_BY_SCRIPT` in audition-scenes.js).

The API documents `condition_on_previous_chunks` (default true) for audio continuity **inside a request**. It is not a previous-conversation-text field. Do not prepend earlier dialogue to Fish's `text`: it would be spoken again. The current separate HTTP requests cannot share that internal audio state. Longer requests or WebSocket contexts need fresh fidelity and latency trials before replacing the measured short-request path. No quality-guard flag or new paid voice training was enabled.

Published Drama3 API pricing is $10 per million characters through November 12, 2026, then $50 per million. This is separate from creator subscription credits. Previous paid S2.1 Pro pricing was $15 per million UTF-8 bytes. The proxy does not calculate user charges from a hard-coded Fish rate. These are published rates, not an invoice measurement. Self-hosting requires a separate hardware and license assessment; open weights do not remove the Community License's restriction on providing a TTS or cloning service to others.

Fish's API schema currently says normal latency and chunk length 300 are defaults, while its prose guide gives balanced and 200. We explicitly send normal latency and treat the API schema/request receipts as authoritative. Its model overview describes broader S2 language support than the older emotion reference. Do not copy S1 examples or stale comments into S2 wiring. Old comments claiming a guaranteed sentence-only cue scope or a currently expired free tier are historical, not current provider contracts.

## Verification and limits

Regression cases must include a mid-piece change, inline reset, one-shot sound, long paragraph, different seat/voice, preview, overlapping calls, and an explicit delivery override. The iPhone and live-call splitters also track the final authored direction, not only the opening one. Their carry limits remain intact. Android already scans all authored tags when carrying between chunks.

Audio trials use identical scripts on both providers at Balanced and Lively. Check words through transcription and inspect audio boundaries/levels; neither test proves that emotion sounds appropriate. Listening remains the acceptance check for warmth, identity, and naturalness. TestFlight compilation also does not prove phone playback. Existing cached/downloaded recordings retain their original performance; fresh synthesis uses the corrections.
