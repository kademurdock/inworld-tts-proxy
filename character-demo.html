A face that follows the conversation.

Listening gestures and expressions run on your device. Mouth movement follows the sound being played, including its pauses.

This is a working rig study, separate from live chat. The drawings are placeholders, not the final Kiana or Lilly portraits. The sample uses a Windows test voice.

What the demonstration proves

The mouth opens with measured audio energy and closes during silence. Expressions start with the scheduled audio. Interrupting or changing characters stops the old visual events. Motion can be disabled without affecting sound.

It does not prove photorealistic animation, phoneme lip sync, production call integration, or native-device performance. Listening gestures are timed movements, not an inference about what you mean.

No account connection, microphone, model call, or GPU service. The face is decorative and does not interrupt a screen reader. All controls work without seeing it.
